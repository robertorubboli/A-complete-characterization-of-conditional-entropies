\section{Lean correspondence}
\label{app:lean-correspondence}

The accompanying Lean 4 development follows the dependency structure of
Sections~4 and~5.  Figure~\ref{fig:lean-dependencies} gives the dependency graph
(arrows point to ingredients and blue boxes mark the standalone boundary
proofs), while Table~\ref{tab:lean-correspondence} records the
statement-by-statement correspondence.  All listed declarations are checked
with Lean~4.32.2 and Mathlib~4.32.2.  The source contains no
\texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, or project-specific \texttt{axiom}; a dedicated
kernel audit prints the axiom dependencies of the public declarations.

The current development formalizes the finite coefficient and
order-theoretic core of the argument.  The general-measure discretization,
the differentiated R\'enyi integral, and the high-dimensional localization
limits remain explicit analytic boundaries rather than hidden assumptions.
Consequently, the assembled parameter theorems expose their curvature
hypotheses in their Lean statements.
